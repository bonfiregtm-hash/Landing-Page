Why replies slip

The reply is the point.So why is it the messiest part?

Outbound works hardest to earn a reply, then leaves it scattered across inboxes and tabs.

Replies everywhere

One inbox per sending address, another for LinkedIn. The interested reply sits unread in the one you didn't check.

Every answer from scratch

Each reply needs reading, sorting and a thoughtful answer. Multiply that by every channel and the hot leads go cold.
