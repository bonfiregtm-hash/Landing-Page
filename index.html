Questions,answered.

01Who is Bonfire GTM for?

Agencies that win on referrals and want a second, steady source of booked calls, for themselves or their clients, without hiring SDRs.

02Do I still need Smartlead, Clay, Apollo and the rest?

No. Domains, inboxes, warm-up, leads, verification, LinkedIn, copy, replies and meetings all run inside Bonfire.

03Will it hurt my main domain?

No. Bonfire only sends from separate domains and inboxes, warms them for 21 days, and pauses if bounces cross your cap.

04Will the AI make things up?

No. Agents write only from your offer, case studies and Knowledge, and anything that changes what goes out waits for your approval.

05Does it do LinkedIn too?

Yes. Connection requests, messages after accept, profile views and InMail run in the same flow as your emails, and replies land in one inbox.

06How fast can I start?

Setup takes days. Warm-up takes 21 days, because cold inboxes are how domains get burned. Your lists, plays and sequences are ready before it ends.

07Can I run it for several clients?

Yes. Each client gets its own workspace with separate leads, plays, campaigns and pipeline.
