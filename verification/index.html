Why verify everything

One bad listcan cost you a domain.

Unverified addresses bounce. Bounces hurt the sender reputation of the inboxes you just spent weeks warming.

Sending blind

The list looks fine until the bounces arrive. By then the damage is done, and the domain you warmed takes the hit.

Verifying by hand

Export, upload to a verifier, download, re-import. Repeat for every list. Skip it once and the invalid rows ship anyway.

Bonfire GTM: verification is a step, not a chore

It runs on every lead, inside the flow, before any email is sent. No exports, no second tool, no list that skips it.

Three outcomes

Verified, held or dropped.Nothing in between.

Every address gets one of three results, and each result decides what happens next.

Verified

The address is deliverable. It moves on to the sequence you approved.

→ sequence

Catch-all

The domain accepts every address, so this one can't be confirmed. It's held back, not sent.

→ held back

Invalid

The address won't deliver. It's dropped before it can bounce.

→ dropped

Built into every flow

Verify is a step.Not an afterthought.

In Flows, Verify email sits between finding a lead and sending to it. Only verified addresses go on to Send email.

Questions,answered.

01Which verifier does Bonfire GTM use?

Reoon, built in. Verification runs inside Bonfire GTM, so there's no separate tool to export to and re-import from.

02When does verification happen?

Before anything sends. The Verifier checks every email before it gets near a sequence, and in Flows, Verify email is a step before Send email.

03What happens to catch-all addresses?

They're held back. A catch-all can't be confirmed as deliverable, so it isn't sent.

04What happens to invalid addresses?

They're dropped before they can bounce.

05What if bounces climb anyway?

The Deliverability guard pauses sending when bounces cross your cap, so one bad batch can't burn a domain.
