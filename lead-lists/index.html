Why lists fail

A list is only as goodas its reason to write.

Most outbound starts with a pile of rows and hopes for the best. The rows don't know who just raised, who's hiring or who just took over the budget.

The scraped list

Thousands of contacts with no reason to write to any of them. Generic copy, high bounces, and a domain that pays for both.

The shared database

The same rows everyone else pulls. Your prospect hears the same pitch from every vendor who filtered the same way.

Bonfire GTM: lists that start with a signal

Every lead fits your ICP and arrives with the reason it's on the list: a funding round, a hiring push, a launch or a new leader. So your first line has something real to say.

Questions,answered.

01Where do the leads come from?

From your ICP and your signals. The Researcher drafts your offer and ideal customer from your words, Scout watches for funding rounds, hiring, launches and new leaders, and List builder finds the people who match.

02Do I still need Prospeo or Clay?

No. Leads, enrichment, signals and verification all run inside Bonfire GTM.

03Can I ask for a specific list?

Yes. Ask Bonfire or List builder in plain words, like “Build a list from hiring signals”, and it shows up as a task you can follow.

04How long until my first list is ready?

About 15 minutes of your time to describe your offer and approve the ICP. Your lists are ready before the 21-day warm-up of your new inboxes ends.

05Is every lead emailed automatically?

No. Every email is verified first, and anything that changes what goes out waits in Needs you for your approval.
