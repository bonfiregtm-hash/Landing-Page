Why domains burn

Most teams learn deliverabilityby burning a domain.

Cold email is unforgiving. One shortcut and the inbox you depend on ends up in spam.

Sending from your main domain

The domain your proposals, invoices and team email rely on becomes your cold-email experiment. One bad week and it's all at risk.

Skipping the warm-up

Brand-new inboxes that send cold on day one look exactly like spam. Cold inboxes are how domains get burned.

Bonfire GTM: protection is built in, not bolted on

Separate domains, a 21-day warm-up, sending caps and an automatic pause on bounces. Your main domain is never touched.

Questions,answered.

01Will it hurt my main domain?

No. Bonfire GTM only sends from separate domains and inboxes, warms them for 21 days, and pauses if bounces cross your cap.

02Why 21 days of warm-up?

Because cold inboxes are how domains get burned. Setup takes days, warm-up takes 21 days, and your lists, plays and sequences are ready before it ends.

03Who sets up the domains and inboxes?

We do. Separate domains and inboxes are set up for you and warmed before they send.

04What happens when bounces climb?

The Deliverability guard pauses sending the moment bounces cross your cap.

05Do I still need Google Workspace and Smartlead?

No. Domains, inboxes, warm-up and sending all run inside Bonfire GTM.
