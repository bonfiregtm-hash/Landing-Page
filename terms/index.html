02The service

Bonfire GTM is AI-powered outbound software, sold as a service (SaaS), for agencies and B2B teams. Depending on your plan and settings, it:

  • sets up sending domains and inboxes, and warms them up over 21 days;
  • builds and verifies lead lists;
  • runs email and LinkedIn sequences;
  • sorts replies and books meetings; and
  • sends you a report every Monday.

The work is done by 8 AI agents. Steps we consider risky are sent to a "Needs you" approval queue, and they do not go ahead until you approve them.

The service is delivered instantly online as a software account. We do not sell or ship any physical goods.
