Why timing wins

Static lists go cold.News goes stale even faster.

A list pulled last quarter doesn't know who just raised, who's hiring a sales team or who has a new CMO. And by the time you spot the announcement yourself, competitors are already in the inbox.

Example

The static list

Exported once, emailed in order. It has no idea what changed since.

  • Row 1Same title as last quarter, maybe the same person
  • Row 2No reason to write today rather than any other day
  • Row 3Generic opener, because there's nothing new to say
Example

Seeing the news yourself

You read about a round, then still have to find the person, check the email and write.

  • Day 0Round announced. Their inbox starts filling up.
  • LaterYou spot it in a newsletter or a feed
  • LaterYou hunt for the right contact and verify the email
  • ThenYou send, behind everyone who moved first

Questions,answered.

01What is a play?

A play is one rule with three parts. When a signal happens (a funding round, hiring, a launch), Who it has to match (industry, company size, geography), and what to Do (find a role, verify the email, send a chosen email). Scout runs it for you.

02Does anything send without my approval?

Matches that need a decision wait for you. You approve or skip each one, and only approved matches move into Leads and the connected flow.

03What happens when an article doesn't name the company?

The match is marked Company unclear and Approve is disabled, because the article doesn't name the company, so it can't be added. You can only skip it.

04How much does a play cost to run?

Plays use credits: 1 credit per search and 0.5 per approve.

05How do plays connect to my sequences?

Each play sends the email you pick, and Open in Flows takes you to the connected flow, so you can edit the steps that follow.
