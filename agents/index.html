Two bad options.You've probably tried one.

The AI SDR bot

One black box does everything. It picks the leads, writes the copy and hits send. You find out what it said when the unsubscribes and bounces roll in, and your domain takes the hit.

The SDR hire

A full salary before the first meeting. Months to ramp. Half the week goes on research, lists and admin, not selling. When they leave, the pipeline leaves with them.

Bonfire GTM: a crew of 8 specialists that ask before they act

Every part of outbound gets its own agent. Each one does one job, shows you its work and waits for your approval before anything goes out. 8 stations, end to end, and you hold the keys.

Questions,answered.

01Is Bonfire GTM just another AI SDR?

No. An AI SDR is one bot doing every job. Bonfire GTM is 8 specialist agents, each with one job: research, signals, lists, verification, copy, deliverability, replies and reporting.

02Can an agent send something without me?

No. Anything that changes what goes out lands in Needs approval first. You approve it, edit it or stop it. Stop all halts every agent at once.

03Will the agents make things up?

They work from what you tell them and your Knowledge. The Researcher drafts your offer and ICP from your words and saves it for your approval before anyone else uses it.

04How long until the crew is working?

About 15 minutes of your time to tell the Researcher what you sell and approve the draft. New inboxes then go through a 21-day warm-up, and the crew builds lists and sequences while they warm.

05Can I build my own agent?

Yes. Under Your agents, add a new agent or build one from a template. It sits next to the crew, with the same chat, tasks and approvals.
