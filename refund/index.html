Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

How to cancel Bonfire GTM, exactly when you get money back, and how to ask for it.

01Summary

The short version

  • Cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of the month you have paid for.
  • 7-day refund on your first payment. First-time Base subscribers can request a full refund within 7 days of their first payment.
  • Dodo Payments handles the money. Payments and refunds are processed by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record.

02How to cancel

  • Cancel from your account settings, or email bonfiregtm@gmail.com from the email address on your account.
  • Cancelling stops your next renewal. You are not charged again.
  • There are no cancellation fees.

03Subscription refunds

The Base plan is USD $149 per month, billed monthly, and includes 1,000 credits per month.

ChargeRefund
First monthly paymentFull refund if you request it within 7 days of that payment.
Renewals after the first monthNot refunded. You keep access until the paid period ends.
Duplicate charges or billing errorsRefunded in full.

04Credit top-ups

A top-up is a one-time purchase of USD $50 for 2,000 credits. Credits are used when agents do work, such as finding, enriching and verifying leads and sending messages.

  • Refundable within 7 days of purchase if none of those 2,000 credits have been used.
  • Used credits are not refundable.

05How to request a refund

  • Email bonfiregtm@gmail.com with the order or invoice ID from your Dodo Payments receipt.
  • We reply within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Dodo Payments is the Merchant of Record and authorised reseller for all Bonfire GTM purchases. It processes payments, handles sales tax, VAT and GST, and issues invoices. By buying, you also accept the Dodo Payments Buyer Terms.

06Suspended accounts

Accounts suspended for breaking the acceptable use rules in our Terms of service are not eligible for refunds.

07Contact
