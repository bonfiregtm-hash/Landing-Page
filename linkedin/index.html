Questions,answered.

01Does Bonfire GTM do LinkedIn too?

Yes. Connection requests, messages after accept, profile views and InMail run in the same flow as your emails, and replies land in one inbox.

02Will it send LinkedIn messages without me?

No. LinkedIn steps run after you approve, and anything that changes what goes out waits in Needs you.

03Can I mix email and LinkedIn in one sequence?

Yes. LinkedIn steps sit in the same flow as email steps, waits and conditions, so one flow can view a profile, send an email, then connect.

04Where do LinkedIn replies go?

Into the same inbox as your email replies. The Reply agent sorts each one and prepares a draft.

05Do I still need HeyReach?

No. LinkedIn runs inside Bonfire GTM, in the same flows as your email.
